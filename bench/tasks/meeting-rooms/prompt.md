The first line of standard input is a count N, followed by N lines
`HH:MM HH:MM`, the start and end time of a meeting on one day (the start is
before the end). A room can hold one meeting at a time, and a meeting that ends
at 10:00 frees its room for a meeting starting at 10:00. Print
`rooms: K`, the fewest rooms that can hold all the meetings. If N is greater
than 0, also print `peak: HH:MM`, the earliest time at which K meetings are in
progress.
