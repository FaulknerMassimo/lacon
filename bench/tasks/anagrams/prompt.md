Standard input contains words separated by whitespace, possibly many per line.
Words are made of letters; compare them in lowercase, and treat repeated words
as one. Two words are anagrams if one is a rearrangement of the other's
letters. Print each group of two or more distinct words that are anagrams of
each other on its own line: the lowercase words sorted alphabetically and
separated by spaces. Order the groups by size descending, then by their first
word. Print nothing if there are no groups.
