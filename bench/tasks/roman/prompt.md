Each line of standard input is either a decimal number (one or more digits) or
a Roman numeral (uppercase `IVXLCDM`). Convert each to the other form and print
it: `14` prints `XIV` and `XIV` prints `14`. Only numbers from 1 to 3999 can be
converted, and a numeral must be in standard form: the form that converting
its value back to a numeral would produce (`IV`, not `IIII`; `XC`, not
`LXXXX`; `MCMXC`, not `MXM`). Print `invalid` for anything else, including
blank lines.
